About Me

"My career has been eventful. From Consulting, Data Analytics, Research, Enterprise Application Security and now AI Governance, Security, Safety and ML Research."

I am an AI practitioner and researcher focused on building, breaking, and governing AI systems. My journey started in Industrial Chemistry, moving through Data Analytics and Consulting, before specializing in Enterprise Security and AI Safety.

Early AI Infrastructure: In 2021, before the mainstream generative AI wave, I managed quality assurance and data labeling pipelines for large-scale ML training systems for top-tier tech companies at Hugo Technologies, contributing to the human infrastructure behind large-scale model development.

My core philosophy is simple: "Whatever we do today will tell how tomorrow will be."

Today, my work spans Applied AI/ML, AI Safety research, and building open-source security tools to ensure the systems we rely on are robust, faithful, and secure.

Currently exploring: Agentic system vulnerabilities and the governance gap between what AI safety research recommends and what organizations actually implement.
