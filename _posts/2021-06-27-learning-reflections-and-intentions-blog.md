**What is the most relevant learning from the Foundation Level of the Forward program for me?**

The Adaptability and Resilience course really helped me understand that I could learn more effectively. Rather than setting performance goals, it is better to set learning intentions instead. Through this, I am now able to approach learning in a different way. Enjoying the process rather than overwhelming myself with high-performance expectations.

**What would I like to focus on in the next month or two?**

I would be working with Python for Machine Learning. I will love to understand the various kinds of models that can be used and how to determine which one would be needed for any project that I would be working on in the future. I also want to apply what I have learnt in the program to my work.
